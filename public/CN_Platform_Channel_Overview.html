KP system (ERP)
  • Single source of truth for pricing and inventory
  • Sync engine pushes updates to each platform server in real time
  • Bonded warehouse: pricing from ERP, stock from bonded WH integration separately
Central OMS
  • Routes all orders from all channels across all 3 platforms
  • Bonded warehouse flow: splits into 2 fulfilment tracks after OMS routing
  • No channel bypass — every order passes through OMS
KP Thailand warehouse
  • Holds all product inventory for KP-CN and Taihaitao TH Direct Mail
  • Handles picking, packing, and export for direct mail flow
  • Replenishes bonded warehouse stock in China periodically
Platform servers (China)
  • kingpower-cn server: hosted in China, receives KP ERP sync
  • Taihaitao server: receives pricing + inventory sync from KP ERP
  • DMALL & JD: integrated via Taihaitao platform, no direct KP integration
